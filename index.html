Open Editor

Everything you need, right in the browser

Professional-grade tools for sequence analysis, all running locally in your browser.

Sequence Editor

Edit linear and circular DNA with a rich visual editor. Undo/redo, find & replace, and real-time complement display.

Plasmid Map

Interactive circular plasmid view with annotations, restriction sites, and ORF display. Click features to navigate.

Sanger Chromatograms

View AB1 and SCF trace files with quality scores, base editing, trim controls, and undo/redo support.

Annotation & ORFs

Auto-annotate from a built-in feature database. Six-frame ORF finder with configurable start codons and minimum length.

In-Silico Cloning

Digest & ligation, Gibson assembly, and Golden Gate assembly. Simulate cloning workflows with multiple source sequences.

NCBI BLAST

Search NCBI databases directly from the editor. Background polling lets you keep working while results load.

Sequence Alignment

Pairwise (global & local) and multiple sequence alignment with conservation visualization, BLOSUM62 protein support, and Clustal export.

Restriction Enzymes

Search hundreds of restriction enzymes. Filter by cut count, overhang type, and commercial availability. Virtual gel electrophoresis.

Privacy First

Everything runs in your browser. Sequences are stored in IndexedDB on your device. No server, no tracking, no account needed.
